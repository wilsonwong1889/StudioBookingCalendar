Studio Community Program

Something new every month.

Once a month — usually the last Saturday — the Hub runs a community program open to everyone: a class, a workshop, a skill session, or a cultural showcase. A new topic every time, led by people from the community.

This month · October 2026

Hip-Hop Dance Class — “BEBOT”

Open to the community
Date Saturday, October 24, 2026
Time 5:00 – 7:00 PM
Location Casa Dance Studio, Lethbridge This month’s program runs off-site, not at the Hub.
Who it’s for Open to the community — come through and bring your crew.
Dance teacher Hyonji · choreography by BROTHERHOOD
Spots 25 only
Cost Contact us to confirm Your spot is secured once payment is received.

Lethbridge, let’s bring the hip-hop vibes. Come learn the iconic “BEBOT” choreography by BROTHERHOOD, Canada’s national dance crew — a track that carried Filipino pride onto the global stage.

Two hours of moves, beat and culture. Come through, bring your crew, and let’s represent.

Fun fact: half of BROTHERHOOD’s original and current members — Canada’s national dance team at the International Dance League — were Filipino.

How it works

Show up. That’s most of it.

One program a month

A new topic, class or activity each month, usually on the last Saturday. Watch this page or our socials for what is next.

Open to the community

Programs are built for community members at every level. Most need no experience and no equipment of your own.

Spots are limited

Sessions are capped so everyone gets real attention from the facilitator. Register early — they do fill.

Stay in the loop
