Thank you to everyone who makes this Hub possible.

The BIPOC Digital Media & Creative Innovation Hub exists because funders, partners, and community organizations chose to invest in creative access in Lethbridge. Their support is what keeps professional studio space within reach of BIPOC creators, entrepreneurs, youth, and artists.
